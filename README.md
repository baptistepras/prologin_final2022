# Prologin Final 2022

My AI player for the final of the Prologin 2022 programming contest, written during the 36 hours of the final. I ranked 38th out of 106 in this first participation, then 18th out of 95 and 23rd out of 112 in the next two.

`Champion.py` is my player. `api.py` and `interface.cc` were provided by Prologin to interface with their game.

## Usage

The player runs inside Stechec2, the client-server match maker developed by Prologin: https://gitlab.com/prologin/tech/tools/stechec2

The report of the final: https://prologin.org/archives/2022/final/report
